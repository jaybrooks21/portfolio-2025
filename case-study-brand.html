Golden Bean Identity System

Rebuilt a coffee roaster’s visual language so it could flex from shelf packaging to playful social drops. Anchored in modular templates and color-coded product tiers.

Section 1 — Basic Info

Role: Senior Designer, Studio Perk

Team: Creative Director, illustrator, project manager

Completed: March 2023

Section 2 — Project Background

Golden Bean asked for a new visual identity that was cohesive across packaging, website, and social. Their previous brand didn’t scale as products evolved. We created a modular design system to keep every touchpoint aligned.

Section 3 — Research & Direction

We audited competitor shelves and saw most brands leaned on earth tones. To stand out, we chose saturated palettes and geometric shapes inspired by coffee origins. Customer interviews told us they wanted clearer tasting notes and roast cues.

Direction: build a system that is instantly recognizable from a distance, flexible for seasonal drops, and legible on small mobile screens.

Section 4 — Design & Rationale

We designed a modular brand system so packaging, web tiles, and banners could reuse the same grid. Bold shapes indicate roast levels; color blocks signal origin. Typography uses a wide grotesk to stay readable on small labels.

Templates live in Figma and export to Canva for the marketing team. Each layout has annotated guidance so non-designers can drop in new SKUs quickly.

Section 5 — Results & Metrics

Reduced design requests by 40%
12 SKUs launched with zero rush fees
+18% click-through on product pages

Section 6 — Bonuses

Featured in Uppercase Magazine, May 2027. Client note: “The new system feels premium and easy to ship. Our team finally feels fast.”
