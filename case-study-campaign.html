Social Launch Toolkit

A launch-ready kit for product drops: editable templates, motion-ready frames, and a messaging map that keeps every channel aligned.

Section 1 — Basic Info

Role: Social Team Lead, BBDC

Team: Strategist, motion designer, copywriter

Completed: August 2019

Section 2 — Project Background

The marketing team needed a fast way to launch seasonal promos across Instagram, LinkedIn, and paid banners. Existing templates didn’t match new brand updates and sizing requirements. We built a consistent kit so any teammate could ship assets in hours instead of days.

Section 3 — Research & Direction

We mapped channel priorities, then analyzed top-performing posts to pinpoint what earned attention: bright color blocking, crisp typography, and succinct CTAs. We also looked at accessibility: minimum contrast ratios and legible text on motion frames.

Direction: create a template matrix covering square, vertical, stories, carousels, and paid display sizes, all with ready-to-swap color tokens and headline styles.

Section 4 — Design & Rationale

We built a template in Figma with nested components for headlines, CTAs, and product callouts. Annotations guide writers on tone and length. Colors are driven by tokens that cascade through variants so a palette swap changes every asset at once.

Motion-ready frames use oversized typography to stop the scroll and maintain readability on small screens. Each asset has space for analytics tags to keep tracking consistent.

Section 5 — Results & Metrics

+24% engagement lift
Team NPS jumped to 9.2
