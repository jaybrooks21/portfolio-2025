Why work with me

Interests & Alignment

I'm drawn to work at the intersection of culture, technology, and experience, particularly where digital platforms support storytelling, community, and access. My interests span Indigenous stories, travel, and cultural exchange, alongside innovative technology, interactive arts, and emerging media. I'm curious about how tools like immersive environments, physical-digital interfaces, and creative technology can deepen engagement without overshadowing the stories themselves. These interests shape how I approach web and digital work: with respect for context, clarity in execution, and care for the people on the other side of the screen.
